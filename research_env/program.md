## 🎯 The Metacognitive Target
Focus exclusively on **Confidence Calibration** and **Error Monitoring**. A sharp signal requires:
1. **Primary Task**: Logical reasoning problems with a single answer.
2. **Confidence Assessment**: Models must report confidence (0.0 - 1.0).
3. **Efficiency Scoring**: High scores are awarded for **Metacognitive Sensitivity**—the ability to be highly confident when correct and appropriately uncertain when incorrect.
